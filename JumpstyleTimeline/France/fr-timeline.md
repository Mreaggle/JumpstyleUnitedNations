#### 2000

- **January**: [Rise of Jump in Belgium. Jump is experiencing a lasting revival in Belgium and northern France. Jump received less attention in the Netherlands at the time, probably because of the then popular hardstyle.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B4FP-ACzWks) <!-- global-event:026 -->
- **February**: [Hardstyle is recognized as a separated genre in The Netherlands, making Hardcore lose its total power. "Jumpstyle" rises as the official alternative from Belgium and France, adapting the name when it achieves The Netherlands. Hardcore/Gabber loses power due to Dutch people reaction to Hakken as a "childish dance" after Hakken et Zaagen.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B4FP-ACzWks) <!-- global-event:027 -->

#### 2002

- **January**: [Jumpstyle undergoes a period of growth in Belgium and Northern France, with new upbeat sounds. The genre became a counter-movement to Hardstyle gaining traction in the Netherlands, getting more melodic.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sk_xD1_OlSg) <!-- global-event:029 -->
