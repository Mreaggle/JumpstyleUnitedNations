### The Undeground/Club | Hardcore/Music Era

#### 1991

- **Context**: "Jump" is a new music genre in Belgium (mainly Antwerp and Lokeren), born as an alternative to Dutch "Hardcore/Gabber" scene, mixing it with another similar genre: the Spanish "Makina" and some Belgium "New-Beat" elements. The purpose is to take out the speed of hardcore and make it cooler for non-hard-dance enjoyers (note: Hakken means Chopping (slicing) as Jumpen means Jumping, in Dutch language). <!-- global-event:000 -->
- **December**: [The first song to be considered "Jump" over dutch Hardcore/Gabber is "SpeedyJ - Pullover"](https://speedyj.bandcamp.com/album/pull-over) <!-- global-event:002 -->
- **December**: [SpeedyJ, from The Netherlands, brings a new perspective on remixing famous hardcore songs to the scene and also produces his own hardcore tracks with bouncy kicks.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speedy_J) <!-- global-event:003 -->

#### 1992

- **February**: [Rotterdam Termination Source - Poing is released.](https://www.discogs.com/release/51080-Rotterdam-Termination-Source-Poing) <!-- global-event:005 -->

#### 1995

- **January**: [Dutch Gabber Hakken dance stills the main one regardless of the music genre being played (Hardcore/Jump).](https://www.nowness.com/story/1995-gabber-documentary) <!-- global-event:014 -->

### The Undeground/Club | Skien/Stamping Era

#### 1998

- **January**: Jump naturally spreads to France, where Belgium acts like a bridge for Netherlands and vice versa. <!-- global-event:023 -->

#### 1999

- **January**: Hardstyle starts raising as a movement and as Netherlands Hardcore/Gabber main alternative, sounding more melodical, atmospheric and easy to listen. <!-- global-event:024 -->

#### 2000

- **January**: [Rise of Jump in Belgium. Jump is experiencing a lasting revival in Belgium and northern France. Jump received less attention in the Netherlands at the time, probably because of the then popular hardstyle.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B4FP-ACzWks) <!-- global-event:026 -->
- **February**: [Hardstyle is recognized as a separated genre in The Netherlands, making Hardcore lose its total power. "Jumpstyle" rises as the official alternative from Belgium and France, adapting the name when it achieves The Netherlands.
