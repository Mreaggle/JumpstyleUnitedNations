### The Undeground/Club | Hardcore/Music Era

#### 1991

- **Context**: "Jump" is a new music genre in Belgium (mainly Antwerp and Lokeren), born as an alternative to Dutch "Hardcore/Gabber" scene, mixing it with another similar genre: the Spanish "Makina" and some Belgium "New-Beat" elements. The purpose is to take out the speed of hardcore and make it cooler for non-hard-dance enjoyers (note: Hakken means Chopping (slicing) as Jumpen means Jumping, in Dutch language). <!-- global-event:000 -->

### Related Footwork and Club-Culture Context

#### 1989

- **During the late 1980s**: [DJ Paul Elstak introduces a raw new house sound at Rotterdam's Parkzicht in 1989, an early anchor for the Dutch gabber culture and its fast `hakken` dance.](https://www.canonvannederland.nl/nl/page/292759/gabber) <!-- global-event:007 -->

#### 1990

- **During the early 1990s**: [Gabber becomes a distinct Rotterdam youth culture with its own visual identity and `hakken` dance, performed with rapid steps to the kick drum.](https://www.immaterieelerfgoed.nl/nl/gabbercultuur) <!-- global-event:008 -->

### The Undeground/Club | Hardcore/Music Era

#### 1991

- **During 1991**: [Speedy J releases "Pull Over" in 1991. Retrospective community histories often treat its bouncy kick as a prototype for the later Jump sound, but the artist's archive does not support the review's former December date.](https://speedyj.bandcamp.com/album/pull-over) <!-- global-event:010 -->
- **During 1991**: [Speedy J's Dutch techno catalogue becomes an important reference point for the harder, bouncing records circulating between the Netherlands and Belgium.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speedy_J) <!-- global-event:011 -->

#### 1992

- **July 11**: [Rotterdam Termination Source's "Poing" enters the Dutch Top 40, reaches number two and stays there for four weeks. Its crossover success makes a raw Rotterdam dancefloor record a national pop-culture event.](https://www.top40.nl/top40-artiesten/rotterdam-termination-source) <!-- global-event:013 -->
