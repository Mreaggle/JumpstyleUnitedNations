# Research Status

Last reviewed: 2026-07-16

## Direct answer about coverage

The recent research pass was substantial but not exhaustive.

The private JUN WhatsApp export was used as a discovery index for names, countries, videos, meetings, forums and first-person context. It was not processed message by message into a complete claim database, and it was intentionally not copied, quoted or committed. The public internet pass verified selected high-value records, especially for Belgium, the Netherlands, France, Poland, Russia and the global revival, but it was not a complete crawl of every language, channel or archive.

## What was covered

- Existing JUN Global Timeline and Key Figures Worldwide.
- Public records from Jumper Records, Ultratop, Dutch Top 40, Qmusic Belgium and VPRO 3voor12.
- Contemporary French cultural coverage from Le Monde.
- Selected YouTube meeting and league records in Poland, Russia, the Netherlands, Belgium, Brazil and the United States.
- Wayback snapshots of JumpstylePolska.pl, Jumpstylers.ru and an archived Jumpstyle Brasil meeting page.
- Public records for Fortnite's Jumpstyle-derived emote and the Yabujin/DJ GYROTTA ZAO revival.
- A first multilingual research vocabulary for all countries represented by JUN timelines and Key Figures.

The source URLs currently promoted by this pass are registered in [`data/source-registry.json`](../data/source-registry.json).

## What remains to extract

### Private discovery material

- Inventory every URL without retaining private message text.
- Deduplicate shortened, mobile and canonical versions of the same link.
- Convert every factual lead into a structured queue with country, person, event and public-source status.
- Identify conflicting spellings, aliases and dates for manual review.
- Mark leads that cannot be published because no public evidence survives.

### Video platforms

- Read title, description and upload date for every referenced video.
- Search channel archives, playlists and reuploads for deleted originals.
- Record event date separately from upload date.
- Expand multilingual meeting queries from 2005 through 2015.
- Search VK, Rutube, Vimeo, Dailymotion and surviving Google Video mirrors.

### Web archives

- Run systematic Wayback CDX inventories for JumpstylePolska.pl and Jumpstylers.ru.
- Recover forum indexes, event calendars, league brackets, tutorial pages and outbound video IDs.
- Map old Jumpstyle Brasil and Orkut-era URLs through archived public pages.
- Locate national portals named by volunteers and Key Figures contributors.

### National histories

- Replace legacy templates with sourced events, one country at a time.
- Prioritize countries with named Key Figures but weak national chronology.
- Add city, organizer, crew and language fields to meeting records.
- Request local review before treating a national summary as mature.

## Recommended execution order

1. **P0 - Source inventory:** normalize all known public URLs and metadata.
2. **P1 - Core countries:** Belgium, Netherlands, Poland, Russia, France and Germany.
3. **P2 - Key Figures countries:** Ukraine, Italy, Brazil, Mexico, Chile and Indonesia.
4. **P3 - Open national archives:** Argentina, Czech Republic, Japan, Lithuania, Malaysia, Spain, United States and Venezuela.
5. **P4 - Cross-check:** review global-to-national assignments, add missing local context and request community review.

## Completion criteria

Research is considered comprehensive only when every usable private lead has a public-source disposition, every promoted video has explicit date handling, every national file is labeled by maturity, broken or deleted links have archive checks, and unresolved conflicts are visible in a public research queue.
